### Auld Lang Syne

Words associated with **Robert Burns** and collected Scottish song tradition; traditional tune. Sung by **Frank C. Stanley**, 1910, Indestructible Record 1267, UCSB cylinder 4410. Exact file and rights: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Auld_Lang_Syne.ogg . Commons identifies this pre-1926 sound recording as **public domain in the USA**. Upstream click removal is credited to **Adam Cuerden**; the page says the unrestored source claims no copyright. This pack retains the provided Ogg bytes and claims no new restoration or worldwide clearance.

Original Scots lyric/context source link: https://www.scottishpoetrylibrary.org.uk/poem/auld-lang-syne/ . **Full lyrics not bundled.** The recording uses adapted English wording and is not an exact reading of the linked Scots original. No modern Commons TimedText adaptation or synchronized captions are bundled; those contributions would require their own license and attribution review. Do not inherit the recording's public-domain status for unrelated subtitle translations or arrangements.
